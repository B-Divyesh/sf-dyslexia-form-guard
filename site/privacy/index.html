POLICY / LOCAL-FIRST

Privacy without fine print.

Effective 27 August 2026

Short version: Form Guard does not collect, transmit, or retain the contents of your forms. Review happens in your browser after you ask for it.

What the extension reads

After you select “Start form review,” the extension reads labels and current values from visible, editable fields in the active tab. It uses those values in memory to show one field at a time, speak a field when you request it, and run local checks. Password fields are always excluded. The values are discarded when the popup closes and are never written to browser storage.

What is stored locally

  • Exact origins you explicitly enable after a sensitive-site pause, including any non-default port.
  • Your Guard+ flagged-first preference.
  • An optional Guard+ license token, its validity, and the time it was last checked.

The website separately stores an optional license token and cached validity in your browser’s local storage. Clearing site or extension data removes those items.

What leaves your device

Form text never leaves your device. If you buy or verify Guard+, your browser connects to the Sociobot billing API and sends the license token only. Hosted checkout is operated by Sociobot with Dodo as merchant of record and is governed by their checkout privacy notices. This site uses no advertising, behavioural analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Permissions

Page access is required to inspect visible form controls only after you start a review and to outline the field currently being reviewed. Storage permission holds the settings listed above. Form Guard does not sell personal information.

Contact and changes

Questions can be filed in the public project issue tracker. Material policy changes will be dated on this page. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.
